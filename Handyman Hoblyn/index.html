About Me

The Person Behind the Work

Josh Hoblyn

Founder

Josh is a qualified carpenter with over five years of hands-on experience in the trade. Originally from Penzance in Cornwall, he brings a strong work ethic shaped by eight years of service in the Royal Navy — where discipline, reliability, and attention to detail became second nature. Now based in Melbourne, Josh founded Handyman Hoblyn to deliver the kind of honest, quality workmanship that homeowners can count on. Whether it's a precision carpentry job or a full fence build, he takes pride in getting it right the first time.
